# Don's Sayings

Donald Miller was known for his witty sayings and philosophical observations. Here are some of his most memorable quotes:

- "You can't win them all, or even hardly any"
- "Lose a few, lose a few"
- "Nothing ventured, nothing gained"
- "Do unto others as they would be done to"
- "Never give a kid an even break, they'll outsmart you every time"
- "Winning is better than losing"
- "I'll make you a deal!"
- "No one here but us peons"
- "Cynicism and pessimism are the only true guides to reality"
- "Politics is the art of compromise, but you never compromise if you don't have to"
- "Every question has two sides, but not necessarily of equal weight"

## Don Miller and his Sayings

For those who spent time with Don Miller, you know that he had many phrases that he used frequently. Some of these are scrolling across the top of the site. In 1975, Don's two daughters, Nancy and Lynne, began to collect these sayings. For Don's 50th birthday in 1977, they presented him with a hand-made leather bound book, entitled *A Conversation with Don Miller*, with almost 100 sayings printed in calligraphy, one per page, accompanied by photographs. For Don's 60th birthday, they "re-" presented the book to him again, with additional sayings they had forgotten the first time. In 1997, for his 70th birthday, they added a few more. At that point there were no more pages in the book.

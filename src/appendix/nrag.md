# Nostalgia Rag

**Composer:** Don Miller

**Copyright:** ©1971, 1991 by Donald G. Miller, Livermore, CA

## About This Composition

"Nostalgia Rag" is an original piano composition by Donald G. Miller, written in the ragtime style. This piece showcases Don's musical talents and his love for ragtime music, a genre popularized in the early 20th century.

The composition is marked "March Tempo" and features the characteristic syncopated rhythms and lively melodies typical of ragtime piano music. Don Miller composed and copyrighted this piece, demonstrating his creative abilities beyond his professional work in chemistry and civic service.

## Musical Details

- **Tempo:** March Tempo
- **Key Signature:** E-flat major
- **Time Signature:** 2/4
- **Pages:** 6 pages of piano score

## Performance Notes

This ragtime piece captures the nostalgic spirit of early American popular music, combining technical piano work with the playful, bouncing rhythms that made ragtime such a beloved musical form.

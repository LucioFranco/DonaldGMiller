# Political Rag

**Composer:** Don Miller

**Dedication:** "Dedicated to Joan Seppala and the staff of the Independent Newspaper, Livermore, CA"

**Copyright:** ©1975, 1994 by Donald G. Miller, Livermore, CA

## About This Composition

"Political Rag" is an original piano composition by Donald G. Miller, dedicated to Joan Seppala and the staff of the Livermore Independent newspaper. This piece reflects Don's deep involvement in local politics and his relationship with the local press.

The dedication to the Independent newspaper staff is particularly meaningful, as the newspaper played an important role in covering local politics in Livermore during Don's active years as a city council member and mayor. This musical tribute combines Don's passion for ragtime music with his commitment to civic engagement.

## Musical Details

- **Tempo:** Firmly (♩= 84)
- **Key Signature:** E-flat major
- **Time Signature:** 2/4
- **Pages:** 8 pages of piano score
- **Sections:** Multiple sections including "Firmly" and "Lightly" marked passages

## Performance Notes

This ragtime piece features classic ragtime syncopation and energy, with sections that alternate between firm, decisive passages and lighter, more playful sections - perhaps reflecting the various moods and aspects of political life.

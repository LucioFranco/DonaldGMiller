# Centennial Rag

**Composer:** Don Miller

**Dedication:** "Dedicated to the Citizens of Livermore, California 1869-1969"

**Copyright:** © Copyright, Donald G. Miller, 1969, Livermore, California

## About This Composition

"Centennial Rag" is an original piano composition by Donald G. Miller, written to commemorate the 100th anniversary of Livermore, California. This piece celebrates the centennial of the city where Don spent much of his life and where he made significant contributions to civic development and local politics.

Composed in 1969 for Livermore's centennial celebration, this ragtime piece represents Don's deep connection to his community and his ability to express civic pride through music. The dedication to all citizens of Livermore demonstrates Don's commitment to public service and community engagement that extended throughout his life.

## Musical Details

- **Tempo:** Do not play fast (♩= 80-88)
- **Key Signature:** D major
- **Time Signature:** 2/4
- **Pages:** 6 pages of piano score
- **Sections:** Multiple sections including "Crisply," "Smoothly," and "With drive" passages

## Performance Notes

Despite being a ragtime piece, the composer specifically notes "Do not play fast," suggesting a more measured, dignified tempo appropriate for a centennial celebration. The piece features multiple contrasting sections that move from crisp, rhythmic passages to smooth, lyrical sections, and culminates with passages marked "With drive" for a rousing finish.
